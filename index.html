DDM and CAPM Valuation Calculator

Cost of equity with CAPM, then a Gordon Growth (DDM) share value. Example numbers are filled in, so replace them with your own.

Sensitivity: value per share

Rows are growth g, columns are cost of equity.

Formulas: cost of equity = risk-free rate + beta x equity risk premium. Value = D0 x (1 + g) / (cost of equity - g). Rule used here: more than 10% upside is Buy, less than -10% is Sell, otherwise Hold. This is a learning tool, not investment advice.
